# K-10 Organizacne centrum

## Ucel
Organizacne ulohy, terminy, planovanie a priradenia v ramci skoly.

## Entity (tabulky, oblast organization)
- `organization_task`: uloha skoly (nazov, popis, stav, termin, platnost/verzia, `is_demo`).
- `organization_assignment`: priradenie pouzivatela k ulohe.
- `organization_plan`: plan skoly (nazov, popis).

## Udalosti
- Registracia handlerov cez `events/handlers.register(bus)` (zatial bez odberu).

## Opravnenia
- Operacie v ramci skoly pre system, vedenie a ucitelov tej istej skoly; ostatne roly zamietnute (deny by default). Kazda operacia je auditovana.

## Rozhranie (API)
- `GET /k10/status` - stav modulu.

## Testy
- Vedenie moze vytvarat/planovat/priradovat, ucitel tej istej skoly moze pracovat s ulohami, ziak zamietnuty: `backend/app/modules/k10_organization_center/tests/test_service.py`.
