# K-05 Znalostna kontinuita

## Ucel
Sledovanie stavu uciva pre ziaka s rozlisenim druhu (prebrate, precvicene, overene) a stavu zvladnutia. Kazda zmena stavu ma dokaz (odkaz na pokus) a platnost; historia sa uchovava ako zaznamy.

## Entity (tabulky, oblast progress)
- `progress_topic_state`: `school_id`, `student_user_id`, `topic_id`, `kind` (`covered`/`practiced`/`verified`), `status` (`in_progress_practice`/`partially_mastered`/`verified_mastery`), `evidence_attempt_ref`, `is_verification_evidence`, `version`, platnost, `is_demo`.

## Pravidla
- Stav `verified_mastery` vzniká iba pri `kind=verified` s overovacim dokazom (`is_verification_evidence=true` a `evidence_attempt_ref`). Precvičovanie nikdy nevedie k `verified_mastery`.
- Kazdy zaznam obsahuje dokaz (referencia na pokus) podla typu stavu.

## Udalosti
- Odoberá udalosti `AnswerEvaluated` / `learning.answer_evaluated` (handler `handle_answer_evaluated`): z payloadu urci druh/stav (precvicovanie vs overovaci pokus) a zapise novy stav ako systemovy principal.

## Opravnenia
- Kontrola cez `core/permissions.authorize` a audit pri operaciach. SELF (ziak) moze zapisovat len vlastne precvicovanie; ucitel v kontexte triedy, vedenie s ucelom, system interne podla pravidiel. Citanie: SELF, GUARDIAN_OF, TEACHES_CLASS, SCHOOL_INDIVIDUAL_WITH_PURPOSE.

## Rozhranie (API)
- `GET /k05/status` - stav modulu.

## Testy
- Pravidla dokazov/verified, pozitivne a negativne opravnenia, spracovanie udalosti (practice vs verification): `backend/app/modules/k05_knowledge_continuity/tests/test_service.py`.
