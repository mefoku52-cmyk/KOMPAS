# K-11 Prevadzkove centrum

## Ucel
Poziadavky na priestory a vybavenie skoly so stavovym tokom spracovania.

## Entity (tabulky, oblast organization)
- `organization_operation_request`: poziadavka (`request_type` room/equipment, nazov, popis, `requester_user_id`, `status`, platnost/verzia, `is_demo`).

## Stavovy tok
- submitted -> approved | rejected | cancelled
- approved -> in_progress | cancelled
- in_progress -> fulfilled | cancelled
- rejected, fulfilled, cancelled su koncove stavy (neplatny prechod vyvola chybu).

## Udalosti
- Registracia handlerov cez `events/handlers.register(bus)` (zatial bez odberu).

## Opravnenia
- Vytvorenie poziadavky a prehlad v ramci skoly pre ucitelov/vedenie (a system); zmeny stavu riadene servisom s autorizaciou a auditom. Neopravneny pristup zamietnuty.

## Rozhranie (API)
- `GET /k11/status` - stav modulu.

## Testy
- Vytvorenie, platne prechody stavov, neplatny prechod a zamietnutie ziaka: `backend/app/modules/k11_operations_center/tests/test_service.py`.
